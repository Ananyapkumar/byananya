## A Fresh Way to Talk to AI
I recently stumbled on **Prelulu**, an app that turns a typical chatbot into a live‑video conversation. Launched on Product Hunt on November 4, 2026, the service lets an AI‑generated character appear on screen, speak, and even mimic facial expressions while you ask questions or tell a story. The interaction feels more like a video call than typing into a text box, and the novelty is enough to make you pause and smile.

## How It Works
Prelulu builds on a proprietary model that powers both the consumer app and a developer‑facing API called **Realtime Avatar**. When you open the app, you select or create an avatar, then start a video call. The AI listens to your voice, processes the input, and streams back a synchronized video of the avatar responding in real time. You can interrupt, change topics, or even ask the avatar to demonstrate a facial expression—everything happens on the fly, not as a pre‑recorded clip.

The underlying technology is similar to other real‑time avatar platforms, but Prelulu distinguishes itself with a consumer‑first design. The UI is minimal: a single button to start a chat, a small toolbar for avatar customization, and a text field for optional typed prompts. The app also supports a “story mode,” where you can co‑author a narrative with the avatar, watching the scene evolve as you both add lines.

## Why It Feels Useful
What strikes me about Prelulu is its blend of playfulness and practicality. For people who miss the spontaneity of a face‑to‑face conversation but are limited to text, this feels like a bridge. It could be handy for language learners who want to practice speaking with a responsive partner, or for creators who need a quick visual prototype of a character without hiring an animator. Because the same model is offered as an API, developers can embed live avatars into games, education tools, or customer‑service portals, expanding the use cases beyond the consumer app.

Overall, Prelulu feels like a delightful experiment that could grow into a useful everyday tool. It’s not a headline‑grabbing AI breakthrough, but it adds a human touch to digital conversation in a way that feels both fresh and accessible.
