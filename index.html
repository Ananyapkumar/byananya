About.

I run, I hike, and I occasionally pretend I'm good at tennis.

I trained as a chemical engineer, which mostly taught me one thing: every process can be made better.

Then I walked into operations. Two years of onboarding clients, untangling CRMs, writing SOPs and keeping teams in France and Canada moving. Somewhere in there I noticed I was doing the same tasks every single week, so I started automating them. Then the automations needed to think, so I started building AI.

Now I want to work beside a founder who's building fast, and make sure the systems, the people and the operations keep up.

Away from the screen, I'm usually on a trail or out for a run, collecting views and kilometres.
